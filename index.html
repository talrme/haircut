These are AI visualizations, so details of your face, hairline, and texture can drift. Lengths are suggested targets, not measurements from the photos. Ask your barber to check your dry wave, crown, and actual length before cutting.

How to read the ratings

Daily effort: 1/5 is simplest, 5/5 is most involved. Change today: 1/5 keeps most length, 5/5 removes a lot. These are practical editorial estimates, not objective scores. Styling times are active time, excluding air-drying and washing; trim intervals are suggested weeks to maintain the shape.

Keeping the long-term options open

For any grow-out goal, preserve front and side length today. A crop, crew cut, or close taper will extend the timeline. Timings start from your photographed length, assume minimal trimming, and use roughly ½ inch of growth per month; wave shrinkage and individual growth vary. Dry length and the ability to tuck or tie are better milestones than a date.

A simple routine for your wave

Condition the lengths, blot gently, and start with a small amount of product. Air-dry when practical. If using a dryer, use low heat and heat protectant. For a wavier finish, scrunch and avoid brushing once dry; for a swept finish, guide with fingers or a vent brush while drying.
